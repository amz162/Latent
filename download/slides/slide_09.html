Product Flow

From signup to executing a goal-based plan in under four minutes.

The full loop, from first launch to a working plan with executable orders.

1
Risk Profiler
~90 Seconds

Seven-question onboarding captures risk tolerance, time horizon, financial goals, and liquidity needs. Algorithmic scoring replaces subjective self-assessment.

2
AI Plan Generation
~60 Seconds

Latent synthesizes 200+ signals per candidate security, then constructs a personalized portfolio plan: target allocation, entry strategy, position sizing, and review cadence. Plans are explainable, not black-box.

3
One-Click Execution
~30 Seconds

Broker-integrated order routing via partner brokers (Singular, Arif Habib, Foundation Securities). The user reviews and approves; the plan executes automatically across the selected broker.

4
Goal Tracking
Ongoing

Daily plan-health monitoring, rebalancing alerts, milestone tracking, and exit triggers. The investor sees goal progress, not just portfolio P&L — purpose re-anchored to outcomes.
