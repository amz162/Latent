Three places Pakistanis go for stock ideas. All three fail them.

forum
WhatsApp tip groups
Herd-sourced tips. No accountability.
Pump-and-dump risk is structural
No risk profiling or suitability
No plan, no exit, no accountability
Often illegal under SECP unlicensed advice rules
show_chart
TradingView + raw charts
Pro tools. Built for traders who already know.
Dumps data, not decisions
70+ indicators with zero synthesis
No Pakistan-specific context (corp actions, tax)
Requires 100+ hours of training to use well
account_balance
Broker apps
Arif Habib, AKD, Foundation — execution venues.
Built for order placement, not advice
No goal-based planning
No risk-calibrated recommendations
UX optimized for traders, not first-time investors
None of them turn “what stock should I buy?” into “here's the plan to reach your goal.”
