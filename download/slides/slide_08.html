The Solution

Latent turns market noise into a plan you can follow.

Latent is not a brokerage. It is an intelligence layer that translates the complexity of the Pakistan Stock Exchange into a clear, personalized action plan — calibrated to each investor's risk profile, time horizon, and financial targets.

01
Signal over noise

Predictive algorithms digest 200+ data sources per candidate stock — corporate filings, technical patterns, macro signals, sentiment, and on-the-ground broker research. Noise is filtered before the investor ever sees the output.

02
Plan over tip

Every output is a goal-based, time-bound, risk-calibrated plan — not a single-stock tip. Allocation sizing, entry strategy, review cadence, and exit triggers are all built in. The investor follows a plan, not a hunch.

03
Personalized over generic

Risk tolerance, time horizon, financial targets, and liquidity constraints drive every recommendation. Two investors with the same capital can receive materially different plans because they have materially different lives.
